PathPilot Driver's Manual

🎯 Objective

Drive your delivery van from the Depot (Start), deliver parcels to EVERY customer, and return to the depot using the shortest total distance possible!

🛣️ Movement & Asymmetric Costs

  • Horizontal Move: 1.0 km
  • Vertical Move: 2.0 km
  • Diagonal Move: 2.5 km (0.5 km faster than 1 horiz + 1 vert = 3.0 km!)
  • Traffic Zones: Multiply road costs by 1.5x or 2.0x!
  • One-Way Streets: Permitted only in the direction of the chevrons.

🎮 Controls

  • Mouse / Touch: Click or tap any adjacent highlighted intersection node.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys or W, A, S, D for cardinal moves.
  • Diagonals: Q (NW), E (NE), Z (SW), C (SE) or Numpad 7/9/1/3.
  • Undo: U or Ctrl+Z.
  • Restart: R.
  • Hint: H (shows next optimal move from current position).

⭐ Star Rating System

  • ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Within 0% of Optimal (exact shortest route found by the solver!).
  • ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Within 10% of Optimal distance.
  • ⭐ 1 Star: Delivered all parcels and returned to depot.
